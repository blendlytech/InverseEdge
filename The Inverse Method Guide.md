## Welcome & Overview

Congratulations on taking the first step toward mastering a proven, strategic approach to the Lottery Pick-3 or Cash-3 Box game. This guide is optimized for human study and **AI Agent ingestion/parsing**, providing a highly structured breakdown of the system, its mathematical principles, and exact play lists.

Success with **The Inverse Method** comes down to establishing the right conditions and knowing when to adjust them. You have the flexibility to fine-tune your play, increasing or decreasing your win ratio based on the exact profit per win you wish to achieve.

### Core Strategic Principle

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Focus on Elimination, Not Prediction**  
> Your goal is not to predict which numbers *will* be drawn. Instead, your objective is to identify which one, two, three, or more digits are **least likely** to be drawn based on current draw patterns. By learning to focus on elimination rather than prediction, you gain a measurable, recurring statistical advantage.

In the Pick-3 or Cash-3 Box game, you win when your three chosen numbers match the draw in **any order** (box play). This allows us to establish clear, mathematically defined profit margins.

*Note: Payout rates differ by state. For consistency, this guide uses the **Kentucky Lottery (USA)** payout structure ($1.00 box play) as our working baseline.*

## Advanced Strategy: Combining Lists (Hedging)

Players can run multiple separate lists with different eliminated digits as a strategic hedge. This portfolio-style approach:

* Diversifies your number coverage across different draws.
* Minimizes exposure to isolated bad draws or unexpected digit spikes.
* Mitigates short-term variance to stabilize your overall profit curve.
